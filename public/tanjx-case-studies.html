INTERPRETATION GUIDE

Understand the result without trusting a black box.

Baseline

The current plan under the selected compound disruption.

P50

The median modeled outcome across the seeded scenario set.

P90 / P95

Conservative outcomes reached in 90% or 95% of modeled draws.

Worst tested

The lowest result in the explicit scenario library—not every possible future.

CVaR

The average consequence inside the modeled tail beyond the selected threshold.

Confidence

Evidence and model confidence, never a guarantee about future events.
