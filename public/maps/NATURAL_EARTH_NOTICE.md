# Natural Earth map data

Maya's offline world land layer uses `ne_110m_land.geojson` from the Natural Earth Vector repository.

- Dataset: Natural Earth 1:110m Physical Vectors — Land
- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Project: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- License: Natural Earth data are in the public domain.
- Retrieved for this concept build: 2026-09-04

The land geometry provides geographic context only. It is not intended for navigation, jurisdictional interpretation, boundary adjudication, or operational routing. Maya's road and rail lines are explicitly synthetic, operationally representative polylines rather than turn-by-turn routes. All logistics, people, prices, quantities, telemetry, risk, and decision records shown by Maya are deterministic synthetic concept data.
